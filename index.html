B

Bandit

A common type of enemy in these lands. Luckily, the bandit is a weak foe who isn't "strong" against anything.

Behemoth

A giant animal which shares alot of similarities with an elephant and mammoth. It's only weakness is pierce damage, since it's thick hide has alot of resistance against elemental attacks.

D

Defender

A large enemy, resembling a giant knight with a big shield. You can't take one of these down unless you use light to blind them. All upsides have downsides and for this enemy it's their eyes. Once you blind them they will self-destruct. Quite gruesome.

Dragon

A creature which is spoken of in old stories, still roaming around the world. Their kind is nearing extinction, but those remaining are more than enough proof to show what though creatures they really are. Their giant scales protect them against all sorts of elemental attacks, and are basically immune to anything. However, pierce attacks are said to be their weakness.

E

Elemental

A creature formed from the elements of the world, basically immune to all attacks. Their body adapts to pierce attacks, shifting their core to another point in their body. Other elements won't hurt them aswell. Their weakness is strike attacks, since they cover a big part of their body.

G

Gnome

A small creature often affiliated with Orcs. Truth be told however: they actually steal from Orcs. The reason they are often affiliated is their weakness, light. Gnomes spend most time underground just like Orcs, so blinding them with light often gives you the opportunity to land the final blow.

Golem

A giant robot type enemy, driven by ancient mechanisms. These old devices served purposes long ago, which are currently unknown as time went on. They are quite tanky because of their thick body but something as simple as a lightning strike will bring them down for good.

K

King

A king of a country far away. Some are wandering around because their kingdoms got destroyed, others abandonded their people. Although they are kings, they aren't strong at all.

M

Mage

A strong foe, using magic and other elements to it's advantage. Mages often use spells but can also use special prayers that summon spirits. The best way to deal with a mage is by using lightning or piercing attacks. They might be strong, but their defense is their biggest flaw.

O

Orc

A creature often affiliated with Gnomes. The Orcs live in caves, often disturbed by the Gnomes stealing their food supplies. Although most of the time they steal lose hairs. It is said that Gnomes use these hairs to make clothing for themselves. The reason they are often affiliated with Gnomes is their weakness, light. Orcs actually can't survive in sunlight and their skin will slowly burn, burning them alive. Nature is truly gruesome.

Q

Queen

Often in search for their husband, the king. Most of them lost hope, and are willing to give up their life if it means finding their king. It's best to put them out of their misery since most of their husbands have abandonded them and most Queens die of hunger in search for their beloved.

S

Siren

An old mystical creature, also called a mermaid. Alot of sailors have gone missing because of these creatures. Don't let their beauty fool you since they are just looking for their next prey. Their demonic nature combined with the fact they mostly live in water makes them very weak to holy and lightning attacks.

Skeleton

The undead legion marches fourth from the dungeons, and somehow they escape to the outside world. When encountering them the best way to get rid of them is by using holy attacks. The best way to lay them to rest is by using the divine light.

Sorceress

Often affiliated with mages, the Sorceress is said to be specialized in ancient techniques and knows how to use them against foes. Although being quite strong they can't properly defend themselves, especially against lightning and pierce attacks.

T

Thief

Sneaky, but predictable. Thieves aren't strong, and are said to come from distant lands by boat. Now they mostly roam around with bandits, looking for victims traveling the lands. Some thieves steal things and trade them for a price, so look out for them.

Troll

An ancient creature which was often enslaved by magic users in the past. They are strong, but quite stupid. Primarily once the Academy of Sorcery found out about their weakness they were used as protection for the Academy.

W

Warlord

Strong foes, the leaders of many armies. Once a warlord retires they often seek people to form alliances with. Do not underestimate a warlord because their knowledge in battle makes them quite tough. Weak to pierce damage.

Witch

Witches, often said to be in league with the devil or the cause of illness. They were often crucified by villagers because of their bad luck. In reality they did use magic, but mainly potions infused with magic to do experiments on nature. Often they were non-harmful, and only wanted peace. Most Witches don't seek fights, but if you do feel the need to fight them use lightning and pierce damage. Their weakness is mostly in sync with other magic users.

Wraith

A creature made from the vengeful spirits of those who have fallen in battle, or suicide. Often filled with hatred these spirits form bigger spirits. They look quite horrific, floating around and haunting people with their scary look. Wraiths are said to haunt people in their dreams. If you want to get rid of a wraith, it is said to use the divine light to lay them to rest for eternity.
